## Technical skills

- **LLM applications:** Python, LangChain, LangGraph, retrieval-augmented generation, ChromaDB and Pydantic
- **Backend and development:** FastAPI, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, Docker Compose and Git
- **Machine learning and analytics:** scikit-learn, Pandas, SQL, Power BI and DAX

## Featured projects

### [Egypt Real Estate RAG Assistant](https://github.com/magedawadallah17-eng/prime-estates)

Search Egyptian property listings by meaning or filter them by location, bedrooms and budget. The Streamlit assistant uses ChromaDB to retrieve listing descriptions and cites the listings behind its answers.

### [AI Career & Assessment Orchestrator](https://github.com/magedawadallah17-eng/sprints-agentic-ai-coursework)

A LangGraph prototype routes learner questions to an advisor or an assessor, keeps track of the conversation and pauses for human review on placement decisions. The demo runs locally with fixtures.

### [MarketPulse](https://github.com/magedawadallah17-eng/Marketing-Pulse)

I analyzed Olist’s seller acquisition funnel and built a three-page Power BI dashboard to explore conversion and drop-off.

## Team experience

During my AI Engineering internship at Sprints, I contributed to knowledge sharing in SprintFlow’s Personal Digital Twin. I tested approval and rejection flows in Mattermost and checked which shared items team members could access.
